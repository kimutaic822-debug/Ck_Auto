CK Auto

Version 1.9 · Build 10
Sell data deals on autopilot

CK Auto turns every M-Pesa payment into a delivered offer. It reads the payment message, runs the USSD menus in the background, chooses Airtime as the payment mode, and confirms the sale to your customer, so you can focus on selling.

What you get

✓ Data deals and free SMS bundles
✓ Works even when the USSD menu changes order
✓ Your own selling prices
✓ SIM 1, SIM 2 or both
✓ Retry, queue and failure reasons for every sale
✓ Promo SMS to your customers
✓ Daily, weekly and monthly statistics
✓ Emergency alerts for low airtime or battery
